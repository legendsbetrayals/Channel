DESCRIPTION
Sparta vs Athens, and the man who fought for both: Alcibiades, the Athenian general who betrayed his city to its deadliest enemy, and then switched sides again.

In the Peloponnesian War, Athens and Sparta fought for control of the Greek world. Alcibiades, raised in the house of Pericles and a friend of Socrates, was the most brilliant and charming man in Athens. In 415 BC he convinced the city to launch its greatest military expedition, against Syracuse in Sicily. Then, accused of sacrilege as the fleet sailed, he fled to Sparta and showed the Spartans how to destroy Athens.

The Sicilian Expedition ended in disaster. But Alcibiades was loyal to no one for long: he fled from Sparta to the Persians, and then returned to Athens as a hero. This ancient Greece history documentary tells the true story behind the Alcibiades of Assassin's Creed Odyssey, and how one man's ambition shaped the war between Sparta and Athens.

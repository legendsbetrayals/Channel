[B-roll]

[Talking Head]
In February 1917, revolution exploded in the capital.
Workers and soldiers rose up.

Tsar Nicholas II was forced to abdicate.
The 300-year-old Romanov rule collapsed in just a few days.
A new Provisional Government took over, promising democracy and freedom.
The Romanov family was placed under house arrest.
At first, many believed they would be sent into exile and allowed to live.

In October 1917, another revolution took place.
The Bolsheviks, led by Vladimir Lenin, seized power.

The Romanov family being moved from place to place under guard. Growing isolation. Strict conditions.
[Talking Head]
The Bolsheviks hated the old monarchy.
They saw the Romanovs as the symbol of everything they wanted to destroy.
The family was moved further east, away from the capital, and placed under tighter control.
Hope of a peaceful exile began to disappear.

[B-roll]

[Talking Head]
The Bolsheviks feared that the Romanovs might be rescued and used as a symbol to unite their enemies.
They made a cold decision:
the entire family must die.

Tsar Nicholas II, his wife Alexandra, their five children, and several loyal servants were all killed.
The Romanov dynasty ended in a blood-stained basement.

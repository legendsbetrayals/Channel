## Script notes (fix in the voice-over if you still can)

1. The hook says the king "looked into the eyes of his most loyal protector and realized he'd been completely stabbed in the back". In the story it is the other way round: York was the loyal one, and the king and queen betrayed him. Suggested line: "...where a king woke from madness, and turned on the one man who had saved his kingdom."
2. "The bloodiest royal civil war in British history" is disputed (the English Civil War of the 1640s killed far more). Safer: "England's bloodiest royal feud".
3. "Edward III died... He had left behind five powerful sons": two of them (the Black Prince and Lionel) died before him. Safer: "Edward III had five sons who grew up to be powerful lords."
4. Henry VI's collapse in August 1453 followed the crushing English defeat at Castillon (July 1453). Bordeaux surrendered later, in October. Safer: "News arrived that England's last army in France had been destroyed at Castillon."
5. "For sixteen months, York ruled": the protectorate lasted from March 1454 to February 1455, about eleven months. Safer: "For almost a year".
6. "Henry stripped York of every single title": York lost the protectorship and the captaincy of Calais, not his dukedom. Safer: "stripped York of his power".
7. The quotes at St Albans ("Hand over Somerset..." and "I will die before I surrender a single lord!") are paraphrases, not recorded words. Introduce them as "York demanded..." and "Henry refused...".
8. Everything else checks out: the 1399 usurpation, Henry V's death from dysentery, the nine-month-old king, the March 1454 protectorate, Christmas 1454 recovery, the Leicester council of May 1455 that excluded York, Warwick's attack through the back streets, Somerset killed outside an inn, Henry wounded in the neck by an arrow and found in a tanner's house, and fewer than about a hundred dead.

DESCRIPTION
Cleopatra, the last queen of Egypt, and the Battle of Actium in 31 BC: the day her ships turned away and Mark Antony followed her, losing the war that decided the Roman world.

The story of Cleopatra begins with Alexander the Great. When his empire was divided, his general Ptolemy took Egypt, and for almost 300 years the Ptolemies ruled the richest kingdom in the Mediterranean. But by Cleopatra's time, Rome was the superpower. To keep her throne, she allied first with Julius Caesar, and after his murder with Mark Antony.

In Rome, Octavian, the future Emperor Augustus, turned that alliance into a war. Antony vs Octavian came down to one sea battle at Actium. In the middle of the fight, Cleopatra's squadron broke away and sailed for Egypt, and Antony abandoned his fleet to follow her. A year later, in Alexandria, Antony died in her arms, and Cleopatra's death made ancient Egypt a province of the Roman Empire.

This Cleopatra documentary tells the Cleopatra history behind the romance of Shakespeare's "Antony and Cleopatra" and Elizabeth Taylor's 1963 film: love, strategy, and the betrayal that ended a kingdom.

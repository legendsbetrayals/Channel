The Gunpowder Plot of 1605: Guy Fawkes and a group of Catholic conspirators hid 36 barrels of gunpowder under Parliament to kill King James I.

They came within hours of succeeding. Led by Robert Catesby, the plotters rented a cellar directly beneath the House of Lords and filled it with gunpowder, ready for the day the King opened Parliament. The blast would have killed James I, his heir and most of England's government in a single moment. Then an anonymous letter reached Lord Monteagle, warning him to stay away. In the early hours of 5 November 1605, guards searched the cellars and found Guy Fawkes standing guard over the barrels.

This is the true story of the Gunpowder Plot: the persecution that drove it, the plan, the letter that betrayed it, and the brutal end of the men behind it. It is why Britain still lights bonfires every year on Bonfire Night and repeats "Remember, remember the fifth of November", and why the Guy Fawkes mask from V for Vendetta became a worldwide symbol of rebellion.

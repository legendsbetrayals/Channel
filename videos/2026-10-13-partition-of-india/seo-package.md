DESCRIPTION
The India Pakistan Partition of 1947 split British India in two. The border was drawn in about five weeks by a British lawyer who had never been to India.

After nearly two centuries of the British Raj, Britain decided to leave India fast. Lord Mountbatten brought independence forward to August 15, 1947. Muhammad Ali Jinnah's Muslim League demanded Pakistan, and Congress, led by Nehru, accepted the split. Cyril Radcliffe arrived in July with outdated maps and was told to divide Punjab and Bengal.

The Radcliffe Line was published two days after independence. Around 15 million people were displaced in one of the largest migrations in history, and estimates of the dead range from several hundred thousand to two million. This documentary traces how the British Empire ended in India, and who paid the price.
